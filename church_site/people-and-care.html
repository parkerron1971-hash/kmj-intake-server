Shepherds

A shepherd for every member family.

Your elders and pastors each take a handful of families. Chief proposes who goes with whom, and you move anyone you like. When you approve, each shepherd gets one email with their families, and finds them on their Lead tab.

No clipboard at the door. A code on the welcome slide opens your church's check-in page, and one tap counts the family, in the building or watching online. Chief reads those Sundays for every family with a shepherd.

Prayer

Prayer reaches someone who'll pray.

Each person chooses who holds their request. A request shared with the prayer team goes to your Intercessor leader, with a first name and the words, due in a day. One kept for the pastors stays with the pastors.

  • Words about someone in danger, abuse or a death always go to the pastor
  • "I prayed", a note from the church, or pass it to an intercessor
  • Emails never carry the request's words
  • The office still sees every request in Prayer and care

Chief

Chief knows who needs a call.

Chief watches every shepherded family's Sundays and writes to the right person on Monday. Ask it who's drifting, who came for the first time, or who has the Carters, and it answers from the same records Shepherding shows.

  • First-time guests nobody has reached since their visit
  • Moves you ask for wait on Shepherding until you approve
  • Never giving, never a note's words, and a child is a first name and an age
