Check-in

Three ways in. One set of rules.

Your team checks children in from the app. A tablet at the welcome desk unlocks with a PIN and locks itself again after 30 minutes. And a kiosk lets a family check in on their own: a parent types their mobile number and taps their children.

  • Name tags print the moment they're in
  • The pickup code is texted to the parent who brought them
  • A brother or sister checked in later keeps the family's code
  • The kiosk can't search names, list families or release anyone
  • Pair a tablet once with a code; retire it with one tap

Pickup

Pickup, checked every time.

At pickup, a leader types the code from the parent's tag or text. They see the children waiting with that code, and the only people those children may go home with: the family's adults and its pickup list. Who took them home goes on the record.

  • A volunteer releases only to someone on the list
  • A family with a custody note waits for a manager to release them
  • Kids leaders see first names and allergies, never medical or custody notes
  • Every leader approved by the office, with the date of their background check

For parents

Sunday, followed home.

Parents switch to the Kids space in your member app. The pickup code waits there while their children are in. After church they find what each child learned, posted by the room's leader: the story, the memory verse and questions for dinner.

  • Past Sundays: the room, and who they went home with
  • Who can pick up, and each child's allergies, in one place
  • Kids mode: the Bible story read aloud, a memory verse game and a drawing pad
  • No messages and no links out. A grown-up holds a button for three seconds to leave

Youth

Teens get a space of their own.

Teens 13 to 17 sign in themselves and land in Youth: their events, their crew, posts from their leaders and a verse for the day. Children under 13 never get a sign-in of their own; they come in through a parent's Kids space.

  • Leaders talk with teens in group chats only, never one to one
  • A group chat with a teen opens only with two adult leaders in it
  • Parents can read their teen's chats, and the teen is told
  • A leader's post is checked before it shows, like every message
  • Its own colour: volt green, or one your church picks

Chief

Chief counts heads, not names.

Ask Chief how many children came on Sunday and it answers from check-in, as numbers only. A child's name, room and notes stay with your Kids team and the office. Only the owner or a manager can ask, and Chief tells you which teams still need a leader.

  • Children checked in, Sunday by Sunday
  • Who leads Kids and Youth, and who they answer to
  • A role you ask for waits as a draft until you save it
