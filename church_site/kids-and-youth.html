  • Who can pick up, and each child's allergies, in one place

Youth

Teens get a space of their own.

Teens 13 to 17 sign in themselves and land in Youth: their events, their crew, posts from their leaders and a verse for the day. Children under 13 never get a sign-in of their own; they come in through a parent's Kids space.

  • Leaders talk with teens in group chats only, never one to one
  • A group chat with a teen opens only with two adult leaders in it
  • Parents can read their teen's chats, and the teen is told
  • A leader's post is checked before it shows, like every message
  • Its own colour: volt green, or one your church picks

Chief

Chief counts heads, not names.

Ask Chief how many children came on Sunday and it answers from check-in, as numbers only. A child's name, room and notes stay with your Kids team and the office. Only the owner or a manager can ask, and Chief tells you which teams still need a leader.

  • Children checked in, Sunday by Sunday
  • Who leads Kids and Youth, and who they answer to
  • A role you ask for waits as a draft until you save it
