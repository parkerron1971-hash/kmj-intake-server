Meet Chief

A chief of staff for your church. It's called Chief.

Chief is the AI built into Solutionist. It works from your church's own records, your people, Sundays, giving and groups, and tells you when it can't confirm something. People make the calls that matter.

ChiefGrace Fellowship · sampleMonday, 8:40 AM
What needs me this week? Said out loud, on a call from the phone
Three things. The Brooks family has missed three Sundays, and Elder Mark has them. Two first-time guests from Sunday haven't heard from anyone yet. And 3 bank rows are waiting for a category.
Draft a welcome note to both guests. Drafted. They wait for you, and nothing goes out until you send them.
Type it or say itType a question, or tap call and talk it through out loud. It's the same Chief on your phone and your computer.
Ready each morningEach morning Chief notes what's waiting for you: drafts to read, people to get back to, what's on today. Churches with shepherds also get a Monday note about families who have missed three Sundays.
Your yes comes firstWhat Chief sends to your people, and anything it does with money, waits for your yes. It takes a kind of task on itself only after you tell it it may.
Shows its workWhat Chief does, including anything that failed, is listed in plain words on your History page. Many changes, like tasks and website text, can be undone for a day. Anything sent or paid can't.

Chief comes with every plan. Why one system

It all shares one list of people.

No exports between apps, no second login for the kids desk, no spreadsheet of who's in which group. Follow one first-time guest:

  1. SundayA first-time guest fills out a card.It lands with the welcome team as someone's job, not a pile on a desk.
  2. Same morningTheir kids check in at the kiosk.The same family the office sees. Tags print, and pickup is checked.
  3. That weekThey join the member app.News, the Bible, sermons and giving, on their phone, in your church's look.
  4. Next monthThey find a small group.The group chat, the prayer wall and this week's study are waiting.
  5. AlwaysTheir shepherd hears if they drift.Chief watches the Sundays of every family with a shepherd and tells the right person, early.
