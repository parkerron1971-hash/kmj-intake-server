Monthly gifts

Givers look after their own gifts.

No calls to the office to change a gift. In the member app, a giver signs in with a code and changes the amount, pauses, stops, or puts in a new card. The card itself stays with Stripe, never with us.

  • Change, pause, resume or stop a monthly gift
  • A new card through Stripe's own secure page
  • Past gifts and receipts in one place

Funds

Funds kept apart. Books that balance.

Givers pick the fund on every plan. On Professional and up, the books keep each restricted fund apart for you, so the building fund never pays the light bill.

  • Your own funds, named the way your church says them
  • Restricted gifts posted to their own line in the books
  • Month-end and year-end close on Professional

Statements

January, handled.

Every giver gets a year-end contribution statement they can file with their taxes, in the form the IRS describes. Chief puts them together for the whole church, and a person presses send.

  • One statement per giver, every gift and fund listed
  • Emailed to every giver at once
  • 990 prep on the Solutionist plan
