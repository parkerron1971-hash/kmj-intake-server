Monthly gifts

Givers look after their own gifts.

No calls to the office to change a gift. In the member app, a giver signs in with a code and changes the amount, pauses, stops, or puts in a new card. The card itself stays with Stripe, never with us.

  • Change, pause, resume or stop a monthly gift
  • A new card through Stripe's own secure page
  • Past gifts and your statement in one place
