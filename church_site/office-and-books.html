Month-end

Close the month in six checks.

One checklist for the month, in order, and each check says what's left. When all six are done, the month locks, so nobody changes October by accident.

  • Each account's statement balance checked against the books
  • The year-end close posts the closing entry for you
  • A year-end package, ready to email to your accountant
  • On the Professional plan and up

Who sees what

Giving stays with the people who keep it.

Who gave what is walled off in the database itself: only the owner, an admin and the church's accountant can see it. Leaders do their work from the member app's Lead tab, which never shows giving.

  • Your church's own titles, from Elders to Trustees, each on a job Chief understands
  • 1 login on Starter, 3 on Professional, 7 on Solutionist
  • A seat for your accountant on Solutionist
  • Every change to a recorded gift is kept, with who made it

Sorting

Chief does the sorting.

Chief goes through the bank rows, matches Stripe payouts to Sunday's giving and suggests a category for the rest. Nothing changes in the books until a person says yes.

  • Matches, categories, and moves between your own accounts
  • When nothing fits, it leaves the row for you instead of guessing
  • It tells you when a month is ready to close
  • On the Professional plan and up
