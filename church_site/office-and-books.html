Month-end

Close the month in six checks.

One checklist for the month, in order, and each check says what's left. When all six are done, the month locks, so nobody changes October by accident.

  • Each account's statement balance checked against the books
  • The year-end close posts the closing entry for you
  • A year-end package, ready to email to your accountant

Sorting

Chief does the sorting.

Chief goes through the bank rows, matches Stripe payouts to the deposits in the bank and suggests a category for the rest. Nothing changes in the books until a person says yes.

  • Matches, categories, and moves between your own accounts
  • When nothing fits, it leaves the row for you instead of guessing
  • It tells you when a month is ready to close
