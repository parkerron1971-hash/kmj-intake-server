Sermons

Every message, kept by series.

Add Sunday's message once: the video or audio link, the passage, a short summary and questions for small groups. It goes up on your site's sermons page and in the member app's Watch tab.

gracefellowship.org/sermons
Rooted in Love
Pastor James · Ephesians 3:14-21 · Sunday, October 25

Paul prays that a church would know a love too wide to measure, and be rooted in it.

For small groups
  1. Where do you feel most rooted right now?
  2. What would change if your group prayed first?
  3. Who could you show that love to this week?

Visitors

Saturday night questions, answered.

A first visit usually starts on the website, late, when the office is closed. Chief answers from your church's details and the FAQ you give it, and takes a name and email when someone would like a hello.

  • Answers only from what's public on your site
  • Never makes up a time or a price
  • Passes anything hard to a person at the church
