Sermons

Every message, kept by series.

Add Sunday's message once: the video or audio link, the passage, a short summary and questions for small groups. It goes up on your site's sermons page and in the member app's Watch tab.

  • YouTube, Vimeo and Facebook videos play right on the page
  • Each message with its scripture, its summary and the rest of its series
  • Chief drafts this week's study for every small group from it, and leaders approve first
  • Members get a note on their phone when a new message is up, once you turn it on

Events

Sign-ups, without the clipboard.

Every event gets a place on your site's events page, with a form people fill in themselves. Volunteers pick the job that still needs hands. News lives on your site too, each post on its own page.

  • Each volunteer role shows how many are needed and how many said yes
  • When an event is full, it says so and the form closes
  • Every sign-up joins the event's list and your church's records
  • The afternoon before, everyone who said yes can get a reminder on their phone

Visitors

Saturday night questions, answered.

A first visit usually starts on the website, late, when the office is closed. Chief answers from what your site already says, and takes a name when someone would like a hello.

  • Answers only from what's public on your site
  • Never makes up a time or a price
  • Passes anything hard to a person at the church
  • On the Professional plan and up
