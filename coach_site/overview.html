Why one system

Every client, one record.

No copying names between a scheduler, a payments app and a spreadsheet. Follow one new client through the system:

  1. MondayThey find you.Your website or a post brings them in, and they land on your list as a new enquiry.
  2. TuesdayThey book a call.From your booking page, at a time you've opened. The confirmation goes out on its own.
  3. After the callThey get a proposal.A program outline or a scope of work, drafted by Chief in your voice for you to send.
  4. That weekThey pay from a link.Straight to your own Stripe account. You hear the moment it's paid.
  5. Every sessionThey keep coming back.A brief from Chief when you ask, a follow-up drafted after, and every step on their record.

Your clients' side

Your clients never need a login.

They book from your page, pay from a link and join class from their own class link. Every step lands on their record, in your office.
