Your week

Next week's posts, ready Thursday.

Every Thursday morning, Chief reads your own numbers, finds the one thing worth fixing, and plans next week around it: five posts, each with a flyer in your colours. Approve them in one tap, change any of them, or skip one.

  • Prices and numbers only from what your business has said
  • Links only to your own site, and no more than three hashtags
  • Change a post after you approve it, and it waits for you again
  • Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X and YouTube
  • On Starter, Chief suggests one post a week; the full week is on Professional

Clips

Your best minutes, cut and captioned.

Upload a recording of a talk, a workshop or a webinar, and Find my best clips cuts its best moments into short vertical clips with captions, each one for you to review. Keep the ones you like, give them a cover, and post them now or later.

  • Covers in story shape for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, and wide for YouTube
  • "Remember this style" gives every later cover the same look
  • Nothing posts until you've approved the clip as it is
  • On Solutionist: ten hours of recordings a month, and up to two of your clips in Chief's week

What it brought

See what brought them in.

Every post carries its own short link on your own site. So you see who clicked, who visited, who got in touch, who booked, and what they paid online. Emails and texts from Outreach are counted the same way, and Chief leans the next week on what worked.

  • Clicks, visits, leads, bookings and payments, over the last 30 days
  • Email and text campaigns Chief drafts in your voice, for clients who've gone quiet, new leads or everyone
  • A thank-you with your review link after a visit, on every plan
  • Win-back, rebook and birthday notes on Professional, in words you approve once
