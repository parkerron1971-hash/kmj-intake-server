  • Profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow and a trial balance on every plan
  • Reports to PDF or CSV, whenever your accountant asks
  • Revenue by client and a 90-day cash forecast on Professional and up

Month-end

Close the month in six checks.

One checklist for the month, in order, and each check says what's left. Close it, and October stays the way it was when you signed off.

  • Each account's statement balance checked against the books
  • After the close, a change needs a reason, or lock the month outright
  • The year-end close posts the closing entry for you
  • On the Professional plan and up

Tax time

Tax time, without the shoebox.

Every receipt stays with the expense it proves. When a client asks for your W-9, it's one download. And in January your accountant gets one package instead of a list of questions.

  • Photograph a receipt and Chief reads the vendor, amount, tax and date. You check it before it's saved.
  • Your W-9 on the official IRS form, filled in from your records, for you to sign
  • 1099 drafts for contractors you paid $600 or more
  • The package and the 1099s on Professional and up

Sorting

Chief does the sorting.

Chief goes through the bank rows, matches deposits to the Stripe payouts your clients paid, and suggests a category for the rest. Nothing changes in the books until you say yes, one row at a time.

  • When nothing fits, it leaves the row for you instead of guessing
  • Turn a category down twice and it stops suggesting it
  • Say “log $40 for parking” and the expense is in the books
  • Sorting on the Professional plan and up
