Invoices

An invoice in one sentence.

Say “invoice Henderson for this month's ten advisory hours.” Chief fills in the price from your price list, numbers the invoice, and adds a pay link from your own Stripe account. Send it by email or by text. The moment your client pays, it's marked paid by itself.

  • A Pay button for card, and PayPal or Square if you take them
  • Repeats weekly, monthly or quarterly, drafted fresh each time
  • Paid by check? Say so, and it's marked paid
  • Void an invoice, and its pay link is switched off too

Packages and retainers

Packages that count themselves down.

Sell a 6-session package and Solutionist keeps the count. Each session marked done draws one down overnight, so nobody counts in their head. Retainers work the same way, in dollars or in hours, and every balance keeps the record of each draw, so you can always see why it says four.

  • A heads-up when a client is down to one session, and when they run out
  • A warning a week before prepaid sessions expire
  • Retainer balances in your weekly brief, lowest first
  • Sell packages on your own store page, with promo codes

Time

Log time the way you'd say it.

“Log 90 minutes on the Henderson matter, drafting the board summary.” Chief records it against the client, rounds up to the tenth of an hour the way time is billed, and works out the amount at your rate. When you're ready, draw it from their retainer or put it on an invoice.

  • Say 90m, 1.5h or 1:30. It's all the same to Chief.
  • Every entry says what the work was, so the bill explains itself
  • Billable or not, and written off when you choose
  • Drawn from a retainer once, never twice

Getting paid

Chief knows who owes what.

Ask which invoices are overdue and who owes the most, and Chief answers from your real numbers. Ask it to chase one, and it drafts the note for you to read. When a client pays, you hear about it, and the payment is already in your books.

  • A final notice, a payment plan, or a write-off: your call
  • Nothing is texted or emailed until you say so
  • A heads-up the moment a client pays, with a thank-you a tap away
  • Refunds, in full or in part, by the owner
