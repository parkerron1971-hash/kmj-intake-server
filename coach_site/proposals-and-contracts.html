Agreements

The paperwork, already filled in.

Pick an agreement and a client, and the names, your business and your terms are filled in for you. The clauses that carry the weight are fixed text that never changes; only the opening paragraph is written in your voice. After the first one, your fee, your state and your cancellation window are remembered.

  • Coaching, consulting, retainer, engagement letter, NDA and more
  • Missing a fee or a scope? Chief asks. It never makes one up.
  • Describe a new agreement and get your own reusable template
  • Add, cut or reword a clause in your own templates

Each agreement carries a short note that it isn't legal advice, and suggests an attorney's eye for the big ones.

Signatures

Signed from a link in their email.

Send any proposal or agreement for signature from Solutionist. Your client gets an email with a signing link and signs on whatever screen they have. The moment they do, you both get the signed copy, and it shows as signed in your documents.

  • Awaiting, signed, declined or expired, in one list
  • The signed copy sent to both of you by email
  • Signed on a Sunday? You know on Sunday.
  • Signing handled by DocuSeal, with its own record of who signed and when

Before it sends

Checked again on the way out.

Drafts wait in Approvals until you say send, and Chief tells you which terms it used, so nothing is vague. Every document is checked again as it leaves: when you send it, make the PDF, or send it to sign. If an edit dropped the signature block or skipped a clause, you hear about it before your client does.

  • Two clients named Marcus? Chief asks which one.
  • A missing fee is a question, never a guess
  • You can still send a flagged document, and that choice is kept on record
