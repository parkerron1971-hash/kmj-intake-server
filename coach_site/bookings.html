The booking page

Booked in a minute. Paid, if you like.

Every practice gets a booking page with its own name, colours and tagline, at its own address, to share as a link or a QR code. A client picks what they are booking, sees only the times you are really free, and leaves a name and an email. No account, no password.

  • Discovery calls, coaching sessions and strategy meetings, each with its own length and price
  • Take payment when they book, or a deposit as a percent or a flat amount, on your own Stripe account
  • Your hours, your days off and a minimum notice, so nobody books you for 7 tomorrow morning
  • It speaks your practice's language: a coach's clients book a session, a consultant's book a meeting

Standing sessions

Every Monday, booked once.

A coaching client who comes every week, or a retainer client with a standing strategy meeting, shouldn't mean twelve bookings by hand. Book the slot once, from your calendar or by asking Chief, and every week lands on the calendar. A week that is already taken is skipped and named, never double-booked.

  • Up to 26 weeks in one go
  • Ten o'clock stays ten o'clock when the clocks change
  • Move one week and the rest stay where they are
  • Cancel from a date onward, and past sessions stay on the record

Your calendar

Keep your calendar. We'll work around it.

Paste the private link to the calendar you already live in, from Google, Outlook, iCloud or Acuity. Its busy times close those hours on your booking page. If Calendly or Square already write into your Google or Outlook calendar, they come along too.

  • Only start and end times are kept: never titles, guests or notes
  • The link is guarded like a password and never shown in full again
  • Checked again every 15 minutes, or sync now
  • Book over a busy time yourself and Chief says so out loud

Reminders

Fewer no-shows. Kinder when they happen.

The moment someone books, a confirmation goes out by email with a calendar invite, and by text if they ticked the box. The day before, a reminder text. If someone still misses, Chief can draft a gentle note offering a new time, and it waits for you.

  • Texts only to clients who said yes, only in the daytime, and STOP always works
  • You hear the moment someone books, on your phone and in the app
  • A no-show fee, if you set one, is shown before they book, and charged only when you press the button

Chief

Say it once. It's booked.

Chief books, moves and cancels on the same calendar your clients book on, through the same checks, so it can't double-book you. Ask it to try a few times first, and it checks them together and offers the free times that day for any that don't fit.

  • Hours, days off and minimum notice, changed in a sentence
  • A taken time comes back with the next free ones
  • Trying times never holds or books anything
