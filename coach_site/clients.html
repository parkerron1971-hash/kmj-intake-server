New enquiries

No enquiry waits all week.

However someone reaches you, a form on your website or a discovery call on your booking page, they land as one person with a score from 0 to 100: what they told you, whether you can reach them, how soon they need you. A form gets a reply drafted in your voice, waiting for your OK.

  • The same person enquiring twice stays one person
  • The warmest enquiries are flagged to you first
  • Nobody answered in an hour? One nudge, naming who has waited longest

Before and after

Walk in ready. Follow up today.

Ask Chief to get you ready, and every session in the next two days gets a short brief: where the client stands, what's outstanding, and one question worth asking. After a session, jot a line of notes and ask for the follow-up: Chief drafts it in your voice. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

  • Briefs are written to you, never sent to the client
  • A follow-up thanks them, recaps one takeaway and says what comes next, signed by you
  • For a missed session, a kind note offering a new time
  • Switch it on, and clients get a thank-you after a session, with your review link

Forms

Ask once, before the first session.

Tell Chief what you need to know, and it builds the form: an intake for new coaching clients, a discovery questionnaire for a consulting enquiry, an application for your group program. Each one has its own page at your website's address, ready to text or email.

  • Intake, discovery, consultation, application, feedback and waitlist forms
  • Answers land on the client's record, and a returning client isn't duplicated
  • Change a question by asking. Nothing reaches anyone until you share the link

Chief

Ask about anyone. Chief has read it all.

Chief reads the same record you see. Ask what's going on with Henderson Co., who has gone quiet, or who has hours you haven't billed, and it answers from their meetings, invoices, messages and time. Ask it to write to someone, and the draft waits for you.

  • Notes you dictate land on the right client
  • Details, tags and statuses changed in a sentence
  • Nothing reaches a client until you send it
