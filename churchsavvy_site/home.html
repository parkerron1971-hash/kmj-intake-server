08Questions

Good to know.

Do our volunteers need an account?No. Volunteers answer asks from the email itself and see their Sunday on a web page. Only the people who plan and run Sunday sign in, and each opens to just their part: planner, worship leader, booth, media or admin.
What do we need for the projector?A computer with ChurchSavvy open in the browser, plugged into your projector by HDMI. ChurchSavvy finds the screen and puts Sunday on it. Other screens open from their own links.
What happens if the internet drops on Sunday?The projector plugged into your computer keeps going, because the booth feeds it directly. Screens opened from a link catch up as soon as they’re back online.
Can we bring our songs from Proclaim?Yes. Bring your song library over from Proclaim, along with SongSelect and ChordPro files. You can also add songs from a photo of the words or by pasting them.
Which Bibles are included?The King James Version and the World English Bible, with search by reference or by words, and verses split into slides for you.
Is there a contract?No. Plans are monthly, one price for the whole church, and you can change or cancel any time.
