We rock at the City.

Here at Love City Church, we thrive on building an atmosphere where change is provoked. We believe if we want to change the world, it starts with you.

The vision

The vision of Love City is to be a place where people of all walks of life can experience the love and glory of God through powerful preaching of the gospel of grace, prophetic revelatory truths, and apostolic empowerment.

  • Discoverpurpose
  • Empowerbelievers
  • DemonstrateGod’s love

Our vision as a ministry is to discover purpose, empower believers, and demonstrate God’s love throughout the world, to expand the kingdom of God in the earth. That’s why we are a loving church, empowered to make a difference in the lives of all people.

The City: eight culture keys

Our culture as a ministry is defined by the letters C.I.T.Y. L.O.V.E.

C
C

Community

2 Chronicles 5:13–14

We believe the community plays a big part when it comes to change. Our focus is to build relationships that are centered around building families, and the right atmosphere for change to be accepted into a person’s life.

I

Intercession and prayer

One part of our foundation as a ministry is the power of intercession. As a ministry we are centered on the importance of prayer and intercession. We rely on the Holy Spirit to fill and empower us as we intercede for those who ask for prayer, as well as the needs God reveals to us.

T

Training and development

Matthew 28:19

We believe in the great commission, as Jesus said: “make disciples.” Our job is not just getting people saved, but also training people to become disciples who go out into the world to make disciples. We believe the call of our ministry is to build the kingdom of God.

Y

Yielding our gifts

We believe that building the kingdom of God takes not only prayer but action. Our challenge is helping people understand the importance of furthering the kingdom by investing time, talent, and even treasure, so the kingdom of God can reach around the world.

L

Leadership integrity

Romans 8:14

We hold to the principle that everyone is a leader. From the ushers to praise and worship, the ministry of leadership is for everyone to play a part in. Our focus is to function as a ministry of integrity, led by the Holy Spirit in everything we do. As the Bible says, “He will lead you to all truth”; the church cannot function in integrity without the leading of the Holy Spirit.

“For all who are being led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God.”

Romans 8:14 (NASB)
O

Operate with excellence

Colossians 3:23

While the Holy Spirit has control of our ministry, we believe excellence is the example of what God desires for His people. It is the demonstration of the Spirit of God in our ministry. One of the key focuses of our ministry is that we flow in the apostolic and prophetic; this is part of our ability to move in excellence. We will never be perfect, but that should never stop us from striving to give God our best.

“Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men.”

Colossians 3:23 (NASB)
V

Voice the true word of God

John 17:17

We believe the true Word of God, the Bible, is the voice of God. God speaks through His word. We are a church that preaches what the Word says, focused on sharing the gospel of Jesus Christ with all walks of life.

“Sanctify them in the truth; Your word is truth.”

John 17:17 (NASB)
E

Evangelism and outreach

Matthew 25:40

Our mission as a ministry is the reason we are a church: we believe every church has one goal, to save souls. We are focused on reaching people of other races, cultures and socio-economic classes, introducing salvation and expanding the Kingdom. Our assignment is also taking care of those in need through outreach, representing God’s love to those in need within our local assembly as well as in the world.
