What to expect

We are a liberated church, which simply means we are free to worship God in the best way we know how.

Our ministry is also a prophetic ministry, where we believe in releasing the word of God as a guiding tool to help direct the plans of God in the lives of others.

Our senior pastors are Pastor Kevin McCloud Jr. and Lady Jessica McCloud.
