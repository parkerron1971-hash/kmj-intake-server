Welcome home.

We are happy that you have decided to take the next step with the Love Nation family. Here is what each step means.

Salvation
Salvation is the free gift that allows us to experience life with God. Anyone believing in their heart and confessing with their mouth that Jesus died for our sins and rose from the dead can be saved. Because of Jesus' sacrifice, we are free from the condemnation of sin. Matthew 19:25–26
Recommitment
Recommitment is realizing that you may have allowed life, sin, people or things to come between you and God, and re-establishing that relationship so you can move forward in your faith and purpose.
Water baptism
Water baptism is an act of faith and obedience to the commands of Christ. It is a public declaration that you are a follower of Christ. Matthew 28:19–20
Membership
As a member of Love City Church, you are part of a family. Membership means committing to worship the Lord together, uplifting one another through encouragement and service, serving inside and outside the church, and holding each other accountable to walk in a manner pleasing to God, as a witness of Christ.
