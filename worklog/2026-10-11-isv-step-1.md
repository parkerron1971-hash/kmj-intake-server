Twilio requires each business that texts to be registered as its own brand
with its own campaign (one campaign for many companies is rejected, error
30926). The plan (docs/plans/ISV_TEXTING_PLAN_2026-10-11.md) lays out the
five steps and six decisions.

Step 1, built here without any Twilio call: each business site now serves
/texting-terms and /texting-privacy in the business's own name;
texting_registration.campaign_words() writes the campaign exactly as
Twilio's reviewers will read it (who sends, who receives, why; samples that
name the business; the opt-in boxes quoted; offers consent apart; the
business's own links); the owner's answers save to texting_registrations
(never an EIN); and a readiness check says what a reviewer will look for.
