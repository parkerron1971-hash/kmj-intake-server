The Reach plan's step 3, first part. Before: the only codes were Stripe
promotion codes typed at checkout (Payments, Discounts), with no link, no
rules about who or when, and no count of what came through them. Now an
offer is a code, a link that opens the booking page with the code
already in, and a printed counter card with its own QR. The booking page
checks it, the checkout takes it off when the visit is paid in full
online, and Grow, Offers counts opens, scans, uses, booked and paid
(solutionist-studio#1194).

Live after merge: apply the migration first, then this backend, then the
app. Refer-a-friend builds on the same table next.
