On a phone the live page is now a room you stay in: the video stays at the top
with LIVE and how long it has run, the chat fills the rest of the screen (no tab
bar over it), and three one-line buttons sit between them: I'm here, Prayer and
Give. Prayer and Give open as sheets over the chat while the service keeps
playing. The chat has the host's newest message pinned, names in colour, one-tap
replies and a heart button that turns into Send once you type; hearts from
everyone float up the side. Turned sideways the video fills the screen and the
chat sits over it; on a computer the video and the buttons sit beside the chat,
with what's coming up under them.

`member_portal_live.render_room` (and its ROOM_CSS / ROOM_JS), `POST
/my/live/heart`, JSON answers for `/my/live/here` and `/my/prayer`, `feed` carries
hearts (and, every sixth poll, how many are here). Tests: `__tests__/test_live.py`
(the room's eight; the switch's test was rehearsed red). Rendered and shot with the
scratchpad harness `livedesign/render_room.py` (phone, sheets, sideways, computer,
white base).
