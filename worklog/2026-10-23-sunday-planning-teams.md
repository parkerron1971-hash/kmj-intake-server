`sunday_planning.py` (19 routes under `/planning`): the Sunday teams with their places, people and who's trained (a team the church already has, or a new one with starting places); its turns; places (add, rename, how many people, retire); who's trained or learning on each place, which also puts them on the team's group; finding a member by name (names only, schedulers only); the schedule (coming services, each place, every ask and answer, who's away); adding the coming Sundays at the church's time; candidates for a place (free and not away first, trained before learning, then the least served that month; teens marked); ask, offer to the whole team, mark an answer given in person, take back; fill the coming weeks fairly; take back a fill. Five server-only tables (RLS on, browser roles revoked) and a trigger that keeps every row inside one church. Volunteers' data rides the account export; the import skips it (it cites ids the import mints anew).
