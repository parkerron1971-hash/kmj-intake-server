Every group now has `/my/groups/<id>` in the member app (`member_portal_groups.py`): Home (what the leader pinned, the newest from the Board, when it meets, the chat and video doors, people), Board (a leader posts, pins one, asks for "Got it" and sees how many have seen it; a member presses "Got it"), and People (full or first names, by the leader's choice). Someone outside sees what the group is, who leads it, and Join or Ask to join; an invite-only group is "not here" to them. A leader answers requests on the group's Home. Connect's cards open the group's page, and Find a group says Ask to join where the leaders say yes. A new phone-alert kind, "Your groups", tells the group about a leader's post, a group's leaders about a request, and the person when a leader says yes; the church switches it on in Settings → Member page like the others.
