Kevin asked whether people given roles should get an assignments tab like the lead pastor's,
or only by level of responsibility (canvas "Who Sees What", board "One Serve tab, three
depths"). His pick: one tab for anyone with a part, called Serve. With CHURCH_AREAS on, the
member app's Lead tab is called Serve and appears for a place on a Sunday team or a kids
room too (`member_portal_lead.parts`), not only a role or a group. Someone whose only part
is serving opens on their Sundays (My serving, rendered in place); someone who serves in
Kids is shown the way to the Kids room; the section switcher offers Serving and Kids room
beside People, Prayer, Families and their group. My serving lives under Serve. Off, it is
the Lead tab exactly as before.

Then the ChurchSavvy door: a part on ChurchSavvy's Sunday team (`worship_team`, by the email the
church has for the member, while WORSHIP_SONGS is on) is a part too. It earns the Serve tab, and
every Serve page carries a door to ChurchSavvy's own app that says the part ("Open the booth ·
Your part on Sunday: the screens and the songs"), including My serving for someone whose only part
is serving. The door is drawn like the Safety room's. It links; it grants nothing.
