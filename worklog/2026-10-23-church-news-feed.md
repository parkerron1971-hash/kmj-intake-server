`church_news.py` holds the records and the office API (GET /church/news, POST /church/news, /{id}/pin, /{id}/remove, /held/{id}, /posters, /settings). `member_portal_news.py` is the member side: Home's "From the church" (the pinned or newest post as a card, then rows) and the bell with how many posts are new; /my/news with the canvas's filters; /my/news/<id> with every reaction, the comments and replies, and "Checking it first…" while a comment is checked. Reactions and Love answer in place (no page jump). Home reads the news beside its other reads (one `home_view` call), so it adds no wait.

Built to the canvas (boards 1–3) and looked at side by side at 390 wide: the pinned post in the church colour with the Pastor tag, the date box, the reaction pills, "Dana, Monique and 47 others reacted", indented replies, "Replying to Dana · Cancel", the sticky comment box with the check line. Checks: 24 tests in `test_church_news.py` (2 sabotages caught: a ministry's post shown to everyone, a held comment shown to others); `church_news_db.mjs` 41 checks on PGlite.
