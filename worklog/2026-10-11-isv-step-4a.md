Once Twilio and the carriers approve a business, its texts have to go out
from a number on its own campaign, in its own sub-account. This moves or
buys that number at approval, records where it lives, and makes sending,
replies, delivery reports and releasing all follow it. The owner is told
"Your texts now go out under {business}" with the number.

The migration (one nullable column and a partial index) is applied live;
it changes nothing until a number has an account_sid.
