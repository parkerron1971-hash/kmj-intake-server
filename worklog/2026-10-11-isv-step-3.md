Twilio requires each business that texts to be registered as its own brand
and campaign. Step 1 built what reviewers check; this builds the sending.

texting_isv.py follows Twilio's ISV API guide request by request: the
business's sub-account, the secondary customer profile (business details,
the authorized representative, the address, assigned to our primary
profile, checked by Twilio's evaluation, submitted), the A2P trust product,
the Low-Volume Standard brand, the Messaging Service, and once the brand is
approved the Low Volume Mixed campaign with the privacy and terms URLs.

texting_registration.py adds the owner's send (marked `submitted` before
anything leaves, so a second press sends nothing), the EIN route into the
business's legal details, Twilio's status ping, a sweep every 15 minutes,
and an update to the owner at every change: in the app (opening Operate,
Text), by push and by email.

Nothing can be sent until TEXTING_REGISTRATION_OPEN names a business; it is
unset, so this changes nothing live today.
