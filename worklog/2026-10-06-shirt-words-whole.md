Kevin's Redo of "Don't Judge Rightness By Feelings" measured 0.904 on the
likeness meter (0.586 before), but his shirt read GOD for GOD IS DOPE. on a grey
patch for a white one. The close-ups crop the print to GOD and the checker
counts white boxes as defects. The drawing instructions, the planner and the
checker now treat clothing words as part of the person: complete and exact in
their colours, or out of view, and a cut-off print sends the design to repair.

Follow-up from the #1294 review: the prompt examples were Kevin's own shirt;
they run for every practitioner, so they are generic now ("HOPE WINS"), and
plain clothing must stay plain.
