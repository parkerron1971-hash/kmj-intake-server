`member_portal_groups.py` adds the Chat tab (/my/groups/<id>/chat): the messages with each person's name the way the group shows names, a quote on a reply, reaction counts, and a tap-to-open picker (Love / Praying / Amen, Reply, Report, and Take down for the group's leaders on others' messages). The page says why someone can't chat yet (messaging off, no birthdate, the guidelines to agree to) instead of a dead end, polls messaging's feed for new messages (never while someone is typing or choosing), and sends without leaving the page. `member_portal_messaging.py` adds POST /my/messages/<thread>/react and /hide, `reply_to` on send, the office's switches on send (`group_board_only`, `leaders_write`), and a `back` field that may only return to a group's chat page. `member_portal_safety.py` offers "Put it back" on a report whose message was taken down. A "New message in <group>" push now opens the group's Chat tab (`member_push.tell_chat`). `msg_reactions` joins the export (before messages) and the import-skip list.
