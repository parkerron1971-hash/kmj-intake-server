gained the doors, This week's cards and the composer (its featured card and its CSS went).

the records; `next_gatherings` (each group's next gathering, one RSVP read), `may_write` and `news_and_reply` are
its reads, run inside `member_portal`'s Home gather. `member_lumen_ui` holds the flat tab bar and the top bar's
CSS; `member_lumen_motion` lost the floating bar.

motion, the 4.1 Home top, the feed, see all posts, Meet the pastor, Light/Dark) and this round's siblings (share,
Connect, posting). `member_lumen_moments.py` is the small moves in Prayer, Give and Messages, not these moments.
