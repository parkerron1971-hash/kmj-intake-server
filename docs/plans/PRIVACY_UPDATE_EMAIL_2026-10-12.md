# Customer email: privacy policy and terms update (draft, not sent)

Send after the policy PR is live, to every account owner, at least 14 days before
the effective date. Kevin decides when it goes. Fill in the date.

**Subject:** An update to how Solutionist handles your data

Hi {first name},

We've updated our Privacy Policy and Terms, effective {date}. Nothing about how
your account works changes today. We're putting in writing what we already do, and
making a few promises firmer.

- **No training on your data.** We don't train AI models on your data or your
  customers' data.
- **How Chief learns.** Chief remembers what you tell it, the rules you approve and
  how you edit its drafts, inside your account. You can see and change all of it on
  the "What Chief knows" page in Settings.
- **Who helps us run the service.** The policy now lists every company that receives
  data to make Solutionist work, including the AI providers, and what each one gets.
- **Patterns across businesses.** Chief can learn from patterns that show up across
  at least three businesses of the same kind, never your messages, customers or
  numbers. It's off for every account right now. When it comes back you can turn it
  off in Settings, and law practices start with it off.
- **Not for health records.** Solutionist isn't built for protected health
  information, so please don't keep clinical or treatment records in it.

Read the full policy at mysolutionist.app/privacy and the terms at
mysolutionist.app/terms. Questions? Just reply to this email.

Kevin
The Solutionist System
