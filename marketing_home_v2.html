Questions people ask first

Will it do something I did not want?

Chief asks before anything that touches money, messages a client, or cannot be taken back. Everything else it does is logged in plain English with an Undo, and Undo means undo: the action is reversed, not just hidden.

Does it actually read my business, or guess?

It reads your real contacts, calendar, invoices and inbox every turn. The morning brief and every answer come from your data, not from a template. Sample data on this page stands in for yours.

My trade is not one of the seven. Does it still fit?

Seven trades arrive already speaking your language. Anything else, you tell Chief what you do and it composes the room: the words, the workflows, the modules, your site. Say what is missing and it builds it in the window, not in a ticket.

What happens when I run out of AI actions?

Only Chief’s AI work pauses, and it tells you. Bookings, invoices, contacts and your site keep working. Top up with a prepaid pack, or wait for the month to roll.

Can I bring the assistant I already use?

Yes. Solutionist is the system of record; whatever assistant you already talk to can work through it, and the log stays in one place.

What does the free trial include?

The whole room, on the plan you pick. __TRIAL_FREE__, no card charged until the trial ends, and you can leave before then with nothing owed.
